# Insurance opinion checklist

Obtain a written broker/insurer opinion addressing technology E&O, cyber/privacy, media/IP,
aerospace/product liability, contractual liability, defence costs, territorial/customer
scope, export/sanctions exclusions, professional services, incident response, limits,
retention, retroactive date, claims-made notice, and whether advisory-only/no-actuation use
is accurately disclosed. Record policy references and expiry in the release evidence.
