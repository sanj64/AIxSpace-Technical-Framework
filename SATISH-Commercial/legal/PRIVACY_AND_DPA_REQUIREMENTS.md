# Privacy notice and support-data DPA requirements

Data minimization target: operator identity, role, disposition time/reason/rationale, and
only the support evidence explicitly approved by the customer. The company must appoint
a privacy officer and document purpose, authority/consent, access, correction, retention,
deletion, processor/subprocessor roles, cross-border access, safeguards, breach response,
data-subject requests, support-bundle redaction, and secure disposal. Raw telemetry is not
personal data by design but may be confidential/export-controlled and is not collected by
the vendor by default. Counsel must assess PIPEDA and applicable provincial/US laws.
