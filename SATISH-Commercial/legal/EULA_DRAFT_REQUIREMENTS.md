# Proprietary EULA and order-form requirements

Counsel must draft an executed EULA/order form covering: company/customer identities;
licensed evaluation or production scope; users/sites/term; delivery and acceptance;
fees/taxes; proprietary ownership; third-party carve-outs; advisory-only and no-actuation
restrictions; customer operational responsibility; no flight-readiness/universal-accuracy
warranty; warranty disclaimer; support/maintenance; limitation of liability; aerospace,
cyber and data risk allocation; indemnities; confidentiality/trade secrets; telemetry and
support-data rights; privacy/DPA; export, sanctions, end-user/end-use/nationality controls;
prohibited government/defence/controlled uses; audit; security incident notice; termination;
return/deletion; governing law; order of precedence; and survival.

Evaluation orders additionally fix the approved dataset, configuration, measured acceptance
workflow, duration, support scope, telemetry handling, results use, and deletion/return.
