# Chain-of-title checklist

- [ ] Selling company legal name, jurisdiction, and ownership of this repository proven.
- [ ] Every employee, founder, contractor, consultant, and document/model author listed.
- [ ] Executed employment invention assignment or IP assignment attached for each person.
- [ ] Source-repository contribution history mapped to people and agreements.
- [ ] Counsel opinion on Apache-2.0 grants already made and dual-licensing authority.
- [ ] Unlicensed polished repository ownership resolved before any code import.
- [ ] Dataset transformations, model artifacts, logos, fonts, graphics, and documentation cleared.
- [ ] Third-party dependency/license and notice obligations approved from complete SBOM.
- [ ] “SATISH” trademark search/clearance and product graphics clearance completed.
- [ ] Patent/FTO and trade-secret/public-disclosure review completed.

Previously granted open-source rights cannot be withdrawn. Proprietary terms apply only
where the company has authority and cannot contradict third-party or retained Apache terms.
