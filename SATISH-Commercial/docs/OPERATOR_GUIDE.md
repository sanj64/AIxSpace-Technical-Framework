# Operator guide

Review the operator card first: what happened, why, advisory response, limitations, and
required disposition. `SAFE_MODE` is visually elevated but remains a contingency
recommendation—not a command. Open analyst evidence when the basis or reference is unclear.

For every non-nominal item, choose accept, reject, or defer; provide the reason code and a
specific rationale. Do not accept merely to clear the queue. In `DEGRADED`, investigate the
failed prerequisite and treat the detector/risk interpretation as unavailable. Follow the
customer’s separately controlled operational procedure for any spacecraft action.

Escalate misleading explanations, suspected artefact/config mismatch, abnormal false-alert
patterns, missing audit records, or unexpected identity/role behavior as an incident.
