# Engineering support policy (contract subject to counsel approval)

Supported releases, hours, response targets, maintenance, and remedies are established by
the executed order/SLA. Default support is offline: verify version/manifest first, minimize
and redact bundles, obtain export/privacy approval before transfer, encrypt in transit and
at rest, restrict access, and delete on the recorded date. No raw telemetry or private key
is requested by default. Critical exploitable vulnerabilities block delivery; security
incidents follow the counsel-approved notification policy.
